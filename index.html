📅 Weekly scoreboard

Weekly head-to-heads use rotating pairs. +5 is awarded when all scheduled games in that week are final; ties award zero. NFL points count only for eligible final games.

🧩 Draft board — all 32 NFL teams

Assign each NFL team to one friend. Team ownership is exclusive. All assignments are saved in this browser until you publish the league file for your group.

👑 Playoff captains

Each friend chooses one of their drafted teams as captain. That team earns 1.5× playoff game-win points. Captain selections should be locked by your commissioner before Wild Card Weekend.

🎖️ Playoff bonuses

Qualification, division wins and byes are commissioner-confirmed (they are not inferred from regular-season standings).

📋 Official league points

Scoring starts at your chosen cutoff. Regular-season games played before that timestamp never count. All postseason rounds of the 2026 season count. A playoff bye doesn't earn Wild Card win points.
Underdog bonuses are commissioner-verified: choosing a team isn't enough. The group must agree on the odds source and pick deadlines beforehand. This tracker does not independently verify betting lines.

⚙️ League setup

Scoring begins Week 11: November 19, 2026 at 12:00 a.m. Eastern. Earlier games never count.

🐶 Underdog picks
